# NestCoins and ShopNest Plus

## Earning NestCoins

NestCoins are ShopNest's loyalty points. You earn 1 NestCoin for every ₹100 you spend on products, calculated on the price you pay after discounts. ShopNest Plus members earn 2 NestCoins for every ₹100. Shipping fees, the Cash on Delivery handling fee and gift card purchases do not earn NestCoins.

NestCoins are credited once the return window for the item closes: 10 days after delivery for electronics and 30 days after delivery for all other items. You can see pending NestCoins under My Account > NestCoins.

## Using NestCoins

1 NestCoin is worth ₹1. You can redeem NestCoins once you have at least 100, and you can pay up to 25% of an order's value with NestCoins. NestCoins can be combined with one other payment method. They cannot be used to buy gift cards.

NestCoins expire 12 months after the date they are credited. NestCoins cannot be transferred to another account or exchanged for cash.

## NestCoins on returns and cancellations

If you return or cancel an item, the NestCoins you would have earned on it are cancelled. NestCoins you used to pay for it are returned to your balance with their original expiry date; if they would expire within 30 days, we extend them by 30 days.

## ShopNest Plus membership

ShopNest Plus costs ₹999 per year or ₹129 per month. Members get:

- Free standard shipping on every order, with no minimum order value
- Free next-day express delivery, and same-day delivery for ₹49
- Free return pickup, with no ₹79 reverse-pickup fee on change-of-mind returns
- 2 NestCoins for every ₹100 spent, instead of 1
- Early access to the Big Nest Sale, 24 hours before everyone else

## Cancelling Plus

You can cancel ShopNest Plus at any time from My Account > Membership. The annual plan is refunded in full if you cancel within 14 days and have not used any Plus benefit. Otherwise your benefits continue until the end of the paid period, and there is no partial refund. Monthly plans can be cancelled before the next renewal date.
