# Domestic Shipping

## Where we deliver

ShopNest delivers to more than 19,000 pincodes across India. Enter your pincode on any product page to see whether the item can be delivered to you and the expected delivery date.

## Delivery times

Standard delivery takes 2–4 business days in metro cities and 3–7 business days in other cities and towns. Deliveries to remote areas, including Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, the North-Eastern states, the Andaman and Nicobar Islands and Lakshadweep, can take an additional 2–4 business days.

Orders placed before 2 PM on a business day are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 2 PM, or on Sundays and public holidays, are dispatched on the next business day.

Large items such as furniture and large appliances are delivered free of charge by our large-item partner within 5–10 business days. Our partner will call you to schedule a delivery slot.

## Shipping charges

Standard shipping is free on orders of ₹499 and above, calculated after discounts. Orders below ₹499 have a shipping fee of ₹49. ShopNest Plus members get free standard shipping on every order, with no minimum order value.

The shipping fee is refunded only if the order is cancelled before dispatch, or if the item was damaged, defective or wrong.

## Tracking your order

Once your order ships, we send a tracking link by SMS, e-mail and WhatsApp. You can also track it under My Orders. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after dispatch.

For orders above ₹10,000, the courier will ask for a delivery OTP sent to your registered mobile number. Please share the OTP only with the delivery agent at the time of handover.

## Delivery attempts

Our courier partners make up to 3 delivery attempts. If all 3 attempts fail, the package is returned to our warehouse. For prepaid orders, the refund is initiated once the package reaches the warehouse; the ₹49 shipping fee, if you paid it, is not refunded.

If 3 Cash on Delivery orders are refused at the doorstep within 6 months, Cash on Delivery may be disabled for your account.

## Delayed or lost shipments

If your order has not arrived within 3 business days after the promised delivery date, contact support. We investigate delayed shipments within 48 hours. If a shipment is confirmed lost in transit, you can choose a free replacement or a full refund, including any shipping fee.

## Faster options

Need it sooner? Express next-day and same-day delivery are available in selected cities. See the Express Delivery policy for cities, cut-off times and fees.
