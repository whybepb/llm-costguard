# Size Guide

## How to measure

Use a soft measuring tape and measure over light clothing. Keep the tape snug but not tight.

- Chest or bust: measure around the fullest part of your chest, under your arms.
- Waist: measure around your natural waistline, just above the belly button.
- Hips: measure around the fullest part of your hips.
- Foot length: stand on a sheet of paper, mark your heel and longest toe, and measure the distance in centimetres.

If you are between two sizes, choose the larger size for a regular fit. Slim-fit styles run about one size smaller, so consider sizing up.

## Bottoms (jeans and trousers)

Bottoms are sized by waist in inches: 28, 30, 32, 34, 36 and 38. For example, a waist measurement of 33 inches means size 34.

## Footwear

India and UK shoe sizes are the same. US men's sizes are 1 more than the UK size, and US women's sizes are 2 more than the UK size.

## Wrong size?

Size and colour exchanges for apparel and footwear are free within 30 days of delivery, and each item can be exchanged once. Keep the tags attached and the item unworn.
