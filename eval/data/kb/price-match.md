# Price Match and Price Drop Protection

## Price Drop Protection

If the price of an item you bought drops on ShopNest within 7 days of your order, we refund the difference to your ShopNest Wallet. The lower price must be for the same item, the same variant (size, colour or storage) and the same seller. Go to My Orders, select the item and tap "Price dropped?". The difference is credited within 24 hours of approval.

Price Drop Protection can be claimed once per item. It does not cover price differences caused by coupons, bank offers, NestCoins or lightning deals.

## Competitor Price Match

ShopNest matches the price of authorised Indian retailers on electronics and home appliances. To qualify, the competitor's offer must be:

- For the same brand, model, colour and storage or capacity
- New and in stock, sold by the retailer itself on its own website or in its own store
- A regular price that anyone can buy at, including the competitor's delivery charges

We do not match:

- Prices from third-party sellers on marketplaces
- Open-box, refurbished or used items
- Bundles, cashback, bank offers or exchange offers
- Flash sales and membership-only prices
- Apparel, footwear and furniture

## How to request a price match

Before buying, start a chat with ShopNest support and share a link or screenshot of the competitor's offer. If it qualifies, we give you a single-use coupon for the difference, valid for 24 hours.

If you have already bought the item, you can request a price match within 48 hours of your order. The difference is credited to your ShopNest Wallet within 24 hours of approval.

## Limits

The maximum price-match difference is ₹10,000 per item. Each customer can make up to 3 price-match requests per calendar month. ShopNest may refuse a price match if the competitor's stock is very limited or the price appears to be an error.

## Sales

Neither Price Drop Protection nor Competitor Price Match applies to orders placed during a Big Nest Sale.
