# Privacy Policy

## What we collect

To run your account and deliver your orders, ShopNest collects:

- Your name, mobile number, e-mail address and delivery addresses
- Your order history, returns and support conversations
- Payment information as secure tokens; we never store your full card number or CVV
- Device and usage information, such as app version, IP address and pages viewed
- Cookies, as described below

## How we use it

We use your data to process and deliver orders, handle returns and refunds, provide customer support, prevent fraud, and personalise product recommendations. You can turn off personalised recommendations under My Account > Privacy. We send marketing messages only if you opt in, and you can opt out at any time from any message or from My Account > Notifications.

## Who we share it with

We share only the data needed for each task with:

- Delivery and logistics partners, to deliver and pick up orders
- Payment processors and banks, to process payments and refunds
- Installation partners, to install products you bought
- Lending partners, only if you choose EMI or cardless EMI

ShopNest never sells your personal data.

## How long we keep it

- Order and invoice records: 8 years, as required by tax law
- Support chats and e-mails: 2 years
- Recordings of support calls: 90 days
- Marketing preferences: until you opt out

## Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent and nominate someone to exercise your rights. Make a request under My Account > Privacy or by e-mailing privacy@shopnest.example. We respond within 30 days. Records that we must keep by law, such as invoices, are kept for the required period even after an erasure request.

## Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and keep your cart, analytics cookies to understand how the site is used, and marketing cookies to show relevant offers. You can turn off analytics and marketing cookies under Cookie Settings at the bottom of any page. Essential cookies cannot be turned off.

## Children

ShopNest accounts are for adults. Users under 18 may use ShopNest only with the involvement of a parent or guardian.

## Grievance Officer

If you have a privacy concern, contact our Grievance Officer at grievance@shopnest.example. We acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and resolve them within 30 days.

## Need more help?

For more information, visit the ShopNest Help Centre or reach our support team on 24x7 chat. We value your trust. ShopNest reserves the right to modify this policy at any time; material changes will be notified by e-mail. Last updated: 1 August 2026.
