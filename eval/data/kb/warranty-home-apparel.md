# Warranty on Home Goods, Apparel and Footwear

## Furniture

All furniture sold on ShopNest carries a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects, such as broken joints, frames or drawer runners. The warranty starts on the delivery date. It does not cover normal wear and tear, fading or stains on upholstery, damage from moving the furniture yourself, or damage caused by moisture.

For furniture warranty claims, a technician visits within 3 business days of your claim to inspect the item. If the defect is confirmed, we repair the item or replace the faulty part free of charge.

## Mattresses

Nestra mattresses carry a 5-year warranty against sagging deeper than 25 mm and against manufacturing defects in the foam or springs. Mattresses from other brands carry the brand's own warranty, shown on the product page. Mattresses cannot be returned once the vacuum seal is broken, so please check the size before opening.

## Kitchen appliances and cookware

Small kitchen appliances, such as mixers, kettles, toasters and air fryers, carry the brand warranty, usually 1–2 years. Pressure cookers and cookware carry the brand warranty, typically 1–5 years. These claims are handled by the brand's service centres.

## Bedding, home decor and apparel

Bedding, home decor and apparel do not carry a warranty. Manufacturing defects in these items are covered by our standard 30-day return window: if an item is defective, return it within 30 days of delivery for a free pickup and a full refund.

## Footwear quality promise

Footwear is covered by a 90-day ShopNest quality promise against sole separation and broken stitching. If either problem appears within 90 days of delivery, raise a request from My Orders and we will replace the pair or refund it. The promise does not cover normal wear, such as worn-out soles, or damage from misuse.

## How to make a claim

Go to My Orders, select the item and tap Warranty claim. Upload clear photos of the defect and of the product label. Our team reviews the claim within 2 business days.

## How this differs from electronics

Electronics have a 10-day return window and are mainly covered by the brand warranty, with optional ShopNest Care. Home goods and apparel have a 30-day return window. See the Warranty on Electronics policy for details.
