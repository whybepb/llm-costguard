# International Shipping

## Countries we ship to

Outside India, ShopNest ships to 7 countries: the United Arab Emirates, Singapore, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Australia and Nepal. We do not currently ship to any other country. Look for the "Ships internationally" label on the product page.

## Shipping fees and delivery times

International shipping is charged per order, for parcels up to 5 kg:

| Destination | Shipping fee | Delivery time |
|---|---|---|
| Nepal | ₹599 | 5–8 business days |
| UAE, Singapore | ₹1,499 | 7–14 business days |
| UK, USA, Canada, Australia | ₹2,499 | 7–14 business days |

Parcels heavier than 5 kg are charged ₹400 for each additional kg. International orders are limited to ₹2,00,000 per order.

## Customs duties and taxes

International orders are shipped duties unpaid. Any customs duties, import taxes and clearance charges are set by the destination country and are paid by the recipient at delivery. ShopNest cannot estimate or refund these charges.

If a parcel is refused because of customs charges, we refund the item price only. International shipping fees are not refunded in this case.

## Items we cannot ship internationally

- Furniture and large appliances
- Standalone lithium batteries and power banks
- Aerosols, perfumes and other liquids
- Any item marked "India only" on the product page

## Paying for international orders

International orders can be paid with Indian or international credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard and American Express) or with UPI. Cash on Delivery and EMI are not available for international orders. All prices are charged in Indian rupees, and your bank may add a foreign-exchange fee.

## International returns

International orders can be returned within the same windows as domestic orders: 30 days from delivery for apparel and home goods, and 10 days for electronics, which can be returned only if defective. You arrange and pay for the return shipping to our returns centre in Bengaluru. The refund covers the item price and excludes the original international shipping fee. International refunds to cards take 7–14 business days after initiation.

Exchanges are not available for international orders.

## Addresses

Please enter the delivery address in English and include a phone number with the country code, so the local courier can reach the recipient.
