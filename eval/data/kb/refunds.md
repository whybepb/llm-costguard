# Refunds

## When a refund is initiated

For returns, the refund is initiated after the returned item passes our quality check, which happens within 2 business days of the item reaching our warehouse. For prepaid orders that you cancel before they are shipped, the refund is initiated immediately. For damaged, defective or wrong items, the refund is initiated as soon as the pickup is completed.

You will receive an SMS and e-mail with the refund amount and a refund reference number. For card and net banking refunds this reference is the ARN (Acquirer Reference Number), which your bank can use to trace the credit.

## Refund timelines by payment method

Once a refund is initiated, the time it takes to reach you depends on how you paid:

| Payment method | Refund timeline after initiation |
|---|---|
| UPI | 1–3 business days |
| Net banking | 3–5 business days |
| Credit or debit card | 5–7 business days |
| Card EMI | 7–10 business days |
| International card | 7–14 business days |
| ShopNest Wallet | within 2 hours |
| Gift card | within 24 hours, back to the same gift card |
| Cash on Delivery | ShopNest Wallet within 2 hours, or bank account via NEFT in 5–7 business days |

Business days exclude Sundays and bank holidays.

## Cash on Delivery refunds

For Cash on Delivery orders, you can choose to receive the refund in your ShopNest Wallet, which is credited within 2 hours, or in your bank account. For a bank refund, add your account number and IFSC under My Account > Refund Details; the NEFT transfer reaches you within 5–7 business days.

## Gift card refunds

Amounts paid with a gift card are credited back to the same gift card within 24 hours. If that gift card has already expired, the amount is added to your ShopNest Wallet instead. ShopNest Wallet balance never expires.

## What is refunded

The refund covers the price you paid for the item after any coupon discount. Some charges are refunded only in certain cases:

- The shipping fee is refunded only if the order is cancelled before dispatch, or if the item was damaged, defective or wrong.
- The ₹40 Cash on Delivery handling fee is not refunded, except when the item was damaged, defective or wrong.
- For change-of-mind returns, the ₹79 reverse-pickup fee is deducted from the refund (waived for ShopNest Plus members).
- NestCoins used on the order are returned to your NestCoins balance, not paid out as money.
- If a bank offer discount was applied, you are refunded the amount you actually paid, not the pre-discount price.

## Refund not received?

Refund timelines for UPI are 1–3 business days, for net banking 3–5 business days and for cards 5–7 business days after the refund is initiated. If the refund has not reached you after this time, first check with your bank using the refund reference number. If the bank cannot find it, contact ShopNest support with your order ID and refund reference number and we will investigate within 48 hours.
