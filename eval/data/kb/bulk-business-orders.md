# ShopNest Business: Bulk and B2B Orders

## Who can use ShopNest Business

ShopNest Business is for companies, schools, offices and resellers that buy in quantity. Register a ShopNest Business account with your company name and GSTIN. Verification usually takes 1 business day.

## GST invoices

Every business order gets a GST invoice with your GSTIN, so you can claim input tax credit. Your GSTIN must be added before you place the order. It cannot be added to an invoice after the invoice has been generated.

## Bulk pricing

An order qualifies for bulk pricing if it has 10 or more units of a single item, or an order value of ₹50,000 or more. Request a quote from the Business dashboard or by e-mailing business@shopnest.example; we reply within 1 business day.

| Order value | Bulk discount |
|---|---|
| ₹50,000 – ₹1,99,999 | 3% |
| ₹2,00,000 – ₹9,99,999 | 5% |
| ₹10,00,000 and above | 7% |

Larger or recurring orders can get custom pricing from your account manager.

## Payment and credit terms

Business orders can be paid by NEFT, RTGS, corporate card or UPI. Verified businesses can apply for net-30 credit terms after 3 successful orders, with a credit limit of up to ₹5,00,000.

## Delivery

Bulk orders are delivered within 7–12 business days. Delivery to several office locations is available for ₹999 per additional address.

## Cancellation

A confirmed bulk order can be cancelled free of charge within 24 hours of confirmation. After 24 hours, a cancellation fee of 5% of the order value applies if the order has not shipped. Bulk orders cannot be cancelled once they have shipped.

## Returns on bulk orders

Change-of-mind returns are not accepted on bulk orders. Damaged, defective, missing or wrong items in a bulk order must be reported within 7 days of delivery, instead of the 48 hours that applies to regular orders, because large deliveries take longer to inspect. We replace or refund the affected units only.

## Corporate gift cards

Companies buying ShopNest gift cards get a 2% discount on gift card orders of ₹1,00,000 or more, and a 4% discount on orders of ₹5,00,000 or more. Corporate gift cards follow the standard gift card terms, including 12 months' validity.
