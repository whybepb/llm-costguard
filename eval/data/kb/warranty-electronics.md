# Warranty on Electronics

## Brand warranty

Most electronics sold on ShopNest come with the manufacturer's (brand) warranty, usually 1 year for phones, laptops, tablets and audio devices, and 1–2 years for televisions and appliances. The warranty period is listed on each product page. Your ShopNest invoice is your proof of purchase, so please keep it; you can download it again any time from My Orders.

Brand warranty claims are handled by the brand's authorised service centres. ShopNest support can help you find the nearest service centre, but the repair decision is made by the brand.

## Nestra warranty

Electronics sold under ShopNest's own brand, Nestra, carry a 1-year ShopNest warranty against manufacturing defects. For Nestra products, ShopNest arranges a free pickup, and the item is repaired or replaced within 10–15 business days. If the item cannot be repaired, we replace it or refund the invoice value.

Nestra batteries are covered for 6 months from delivery.

## ShopNest Care extended warranty

ShopNest Care extends the warranty on electronics priced ₹2,000 and above. It must be bought within 30 days of delivery of the product.

- 1 extra year of warranty costs 7% of the product price.
- 2 extra years of warranty cost 12% of the product price.
- An Accidental and Liquid Damage plan costs 9% of the product price for 1 year and covers one claim.

ShopNest Care starts when the brand warranty ends and covers the same manufacturing defects. It can be cancelled within 15 days of purchase for a full refund, if no claim has been made.

## What warranty does not cover

Warranty, including ShopNest Care, does not cover:

- Physical damage such as cracked screens or dents, unless you have the Accidental and Liquid Damage plan
- Liquid damage, unless you have the Accidental and Liquid Damage plan
- Damage from repairs by unauthorised service centres
- Software problems, viruses and data loss
- Cosmetic wear and tear

Please back up your data before sending a device for repair. ShopNest is not responsible for data lost during repair.

## Dead on arrival or defective within 10 days

If an electronics item does not work when you first use it, or develops a defect within 10 days of delivery, you do not need to go through warranty. Raise a return or replacement request from My Orders within 10 days of delivery and we will collect it for free. After 10 days, defects are handled through the brand warranty or ShopNest Care.

## How to make a warranty claim

For Nestra products and ShopNest Care plans, go to My Orders, select the item and tap Warranty claim. Describe the problem and add photos or a short video. Our service team responds within 1 business day.
