# Account and Security

## Signing in

You can sign in to ShopNest with a one-time password (OTP) sent to your mobile number, or with your e-mail address and password. We recommend turning on 2-step verification under My Account > Security, so that a new device needs both your password and an OTP.

Each mobile number can be linked to only one ShopNest account.

## Locked accounts

After 5 failed sign-in attempts, your account is locked for 30 minutes to protect it. You can wait 30 minutes and try again, or reset your password straight away using the "Forgot password" link, which sends a reset link to your registered e-mail address. The reset link is valid for 24 hours.

## Changing your mobile number

To change the mobile number on your account, go to My Account > Profile > Mobile number. We send an OTP to both your old and your new number. If you no longer have access to the old number, choose "I don't have my old number" and we will verify you through a link sent to your registered e-mail address, valid for 24 hours.

## Staying safe

Important: ShopNest will never ask for your OTP, password, CVV, UPI PIN or full card number over phone, e-mail, SMS or chat. Our agents will never ask you to install a screen-sharing or remote-access app. If someone asks for these details claiming to be from ShopNest, do not share them and report it to security@shopnest.example.

Beware of fake ShopNest pages and messages offering prizes, refunds or jobs in exchange for a payment or your bank details. ShopNest never asks you to pay money to receive a refund.

## Unauthorised activity

If you see an order you did not place, or a change you did not make, report it within 24 hours through chat or by calling 1800-202-6378. We freeze the account within 1 hour of your report and complete the investigation within 7 business days. Any amount charged for a confirmed unauthorised order is refunded in full.

You can sign out of all devices at any time from My Account > Security > Sign out everywhere.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account from My Account > Privacy > Delete account. Deletion is completed within 30 days of your request. Before you delete it:

- All orders must be delivered and all returns and refunds completed.
- Your NestCoins are forfeited and cannot be restored.
- Your ShopNest Wallet balance is refunded to your bank account.
- Order and invoice records are kept for 8 years, as required by tax law, even after deletion.

## Need more help?

For more information, visit the ShopNest Help Centre or reach our support team on 24x7 chat. We value your business and thank you for shopping with ShopNest. Last updated: 1 August 2026.
