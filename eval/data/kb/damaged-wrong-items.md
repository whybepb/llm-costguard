# Damaged, Defective, Missing or Wrong Items

## Report within 48 hours

If your order arrives damaged, defective, incomplete or different from what you ordered, report it within 48 hours of delivery. Go to My Orders, select the item and tap "Report a problem". Add clear photos of the item, the packaging and the shipping label.

For electronics priced ₹10,000 and above, an unboxing video is also required. The video must be continuous, start with the sealed package showing the shipping label, and show the product being taken out and switched on.

## What we do

For damaged, defective or wrong items, you can choose a free replacement or a full refund. The pickup is free, and the refund includes the shipping fee and the ₹40 Cash on Delivery handling fee, if you paid them. Refunds for damaged or wrong items are initiated as soon as the pickup is completed.

Refunds reach UPI accounts in 1–3 business days, net banking in 3–5 business days and credit or debit cards in 5–7 business days after initiation. ShopNest Wallet refunds arrive within 2 hours.

## Wrong item delivered

If you received a different product, size or colour from the one you ordered, please do not use it or remove its tags. We will pick it up and ship the correct item within 3–5 business days. If the correct item is out of stock, you receive a full refund.

## Missing items or accessories

If an item or an accessory is missing, report it within 48 hours of delivery. Before you report it, check whether your order was split into several shipments; My Orders shows each shipment separately. We review missing-item reports within 2 business days, and then ship the missing item or refund it.

## Damaged packaging at delivery

If the outer box is visibly damaged, torn or wet when the courier arrives, you can refuse the delivery, or accept it and ask the courier to note the damage on their device. Either way, please report it to us within 48 hours with photos.

## Large appliances

Do not unbox large appliances such as refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners and televisions yourself. Our installation technician unboxes and checks the appliance during the installation visit. Any damage the technician finds at unboxing is treated as reported within 48 hours, even if the visit happens later.

## Reported after 48 hours

Problems reported more than 48 hours after delivery are handled as a standard return, if the item is still within its return window (30 days for most items, 10 days for electronics), or through the warranty.

## Bulk orders

Bulk and business orders have a longer reporting window of 7 days; see the ShopNest Business policy.
