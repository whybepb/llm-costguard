# Cash on Delivery and EMI

## Cash on Delivery (COD)

Cash on Delivery is available on orders up to ₹50,000 delivered within India. A COD handling fee of ₹40 is added to each COD order. You can pay the delivery agent in cash or by scanning the agent's UPI QR code at the doorstep. Please keep the exact amount ready where possible.

Cash on Delivery is not available for:

- Orders above ₹50,000
- Gift card purchases
- International orders
- Express orders above ₹10,000

If 3 Cash on Delivery orders are refused at the doorstep within 6 months, COD may be disabled for your account. You can still pay online.

The ₹40 COD handling fee is not refunded on returns or refusals, except when the item was damaged, defective or wrong.

## Refunds for COD orders

For COD orders, refunds go either to your ShopNest Wallet, credited within 2 hours, or to your bank account by NEFT within 5–7 business days after you add your account number and IFSC. COD refunds cannot be paid out in cash.

## Standard card EMI

EMI (equated monthly instalments) is available on credit cards from most major banks for orders of ₹3,000 and above. You can choose a tenure of 3, 6, 9, 12, 18 or 24 months. Interest is charged by your bank, typically 13–16% per year, and appears on your card statement. The full order amount is blocked on your card limit and released as you pay each instalment.

## No-cost EMI

No-cost EMI is available on orders of ₹10,000 and above with select bank credit cards, for tenures of 3 and 6 months. ShopNest gives an upfront discount equal to the interest your bank charges, so you pay only the product price over the tenure. Your bank may charge a one-time processing fee of up to ₹199 plus GST, which is not refunded.

## Debit card and cardless EMI

Debit card EMI is available to pre-approved customers of select banks; your eligibility is shown at checkout. Cardless EMI through our partner lenders is available on orders between ₹5,000 and ₹1,00,000, subject to a quick eligibility check.

## EMI on cancellations and returns

If you cancel or return an order paid by card EMI, the refund goes back to your card and your bank cancels the EMI plan. Card EMI refunds take 7–10 business days after the refund is initiated. Interest already charged by your bank for the months that have passed, and any processing fee, are governed by your bank's policy and are not refunded by ShopNest.

EMI is not available on gift card purchases or international orders.
