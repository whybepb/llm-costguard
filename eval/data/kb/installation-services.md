# Installation Services

## Free installation

ShopNest offers free installation or a free demo for these products:

- Televisions: free wall-mount or table-top installation. A wall-mount bracket is included free for televisions of 43 inches and above; for smaller televisions a bracket costs ₹499.
- Washing machines, refrigerators and dishwashers: free installation and demo.
- Water purifiers: free installation, including standard fittings.

## Paid installation

| Service | Charge |
|---|---|
| Split air conditioner installation | ₹1,499 |
| Window air conditioner installation | ₹799 |
| Extra copper pipe beyond 3 metres | ₹650 per metre |
| Outdoor unit stand | ₹600 |
| Uninstalling an old air conditioner | ₹500 |
| Kitchen chimney installation | ₹999 |

Air conditioner installation includes up to 3 metres of copper pipe. Charges are paid online when you book, or to the technician by UPI, who will give you a receipt. Please do not pay any charge without a receipt.

## Furniture assembly

Furniture assembly is free for furniture priced ₹5,000 and above. For furniture priced below ₹5,000, assembly costs ₹499. Furniture that needs no assembly is marked "Ready to use" on the product page.

## Scheduling

Installation is booked automatically when your order is delivered. A technician visits within 48 hours of delivery in metro cities and within 3–5 days in other cities. You will receive the technician's name and visit time by SMS.

You can reschedule an installation up to 2 times free of charge from My Orders. If the technician arrives at the booked time and nobody is available, and the visit was not rescheduled, a ₹199 revisit fee applies.

## Do not unbox large appliances

Please do not unbox televisions, refrigerators, washing machines or air conditioners before the technician arrives. The technician unboxes the product and checks it in front of you. Any damage found at unboxing is treated as reported within 48 hours of delivery, and we replace the product free of charge.

## Where installation is available

Installation services are available only for deliveries within India, in pincodes where ShopNest delivers. They are not available for international orders.
