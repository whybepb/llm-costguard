# Payment Methods

## Accepted payment methods

ShopNest accepts the following payment methods on orders delivered within India:

- UPI, using any UPI app, a UPI ID or a QR code
- Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express and Diners Club
- Net banking from more than 50 banks
- ShopNest Wallet balance
- ShopNest gift cards
- NestCoins (1 NestCoin = ₹1)
- EMI on credit cards, select debit cards and cardless EMI
- Cash on Delivery (COD)

All prices are in Indian rupees (INR) and include GST.

## UPI

UPI payments are confirmed instantly. The maximum amount per UPI transaction is set by your bank, usually ₹1,00,000. ShopNest Plus membership can be renewed automatically using UPI AutoPay.

## Cards

Saved cards are stored as secure tokens in line with RBI card-tokenisation rules. ShopNest never stores your full card number or your CVV. Every card payment is authenticated with an OTP or your bank's 3D Secure page. International cards are accepted, and are charged in Indian rupees; your bank may add a foreign-exchange fee.

## Combining payment methods

You can split a payment between ShopNest Wallet balance, gift cards and NestCoins, and one other payment method such as UPI or a card. You cannot split a payment between two cards or between a card and UPI.

## Failed payments

If money was debited but your order was not confirmed, the amount is reversed automatically. UPI debits are reversed within 48 hours. Card and net banking debits are reversed within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank. You do not need to contact us; you can place the order again at any time.

If a payment fails repeatedly, try another method or clear your browser cache. Do not share screenshots of card details with anyone claiming to help with a failed payment.

## Invoices

A GST invoice is generated for every order when it ships. Download it from My Orders > Order details > Download invoice. Business customers who need GST input tax credit must add their GSTIN before placing the order; see the ShopNest Business policy.

## Payment security

Important: ShopNest will never ask for your OTP, password, CVV, UPI PIN or full card number over phone, e-mail, SMS or chat. If someone asks for these details claiming to be from ShopNest, do not share them and report it to security@shopnest.example.
