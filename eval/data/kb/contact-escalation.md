# Contact Us and Escalation

## How to reach us

- Chat: 24x7 in the ShopNest app and on the website, under Help.
- Phone: 1800-202-6378 (toll-free), 8 AM to 10 PM IST, every day including Sundays and public holidays.
- E-mail: support@shopnest.example. We reply within 24 hours.

Support is available in English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and Bengali. Choose your language at the start of the chat or call.

You can also request a callback from the Help page. During phone support hours, we call back within 2 hours.

## Your ticket number

Every request gets a ticket number in the format SN- followed by 8 digits, for example SN-20461183. Keep this number; it lets any agent see the full history of your request, so you do not need to explain it again.

## Escalation levels

If your issue is not resolved, you can escalate it in three steps:

1. **Level 1, Customer Support:** chat, phone or e-mail. Most issues are resolved here.
2. **Level 2, Customer Care Manager:** if your issue is not resolved within 48 hours, ask the agent to escalate it, or tap "Escalate" on your ticket. A Customer Care Manager responds within 1 business day and aims to resolve the issue within 3 business days.
3. **Level 3, Grievance Officer:** if you are still not satisfied, write to grievance@shopnest.example with your ticket number. The Grievance Officer acknowledges your complaint within 48 hours and resolves it within 30 days, as required by the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

If your complaint is still unresolved after these steps, you can contact the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915.

## Staying safe when you contact us

ShopNest will never ask for your OTP, password, CVV, UPI PIN or full card number over phone, e-mail, SMS or chat. Our official toll-free number is 1800-202-6378; we do not provide support through personal mobile numbers or social media direct messages.

Please do not post order numbers, phone numbers or addresses publicly on social media. If you contact us on social media, we will move the conversation to a private channel.

## Business and press enquiries

For bulk and business orders, e-mail business@shopnest.example. For privacy requests, e-mail privacy@shopnest.example.
