# Returns Policy

## Return windows

ShopNest accepts returns on most items within 30 days of delivery. This 30-day window applies to apparel, footwear, home goods, kitchenware, bedding, home decor and furniture.

Electronics have a shorter return window of 10 days from delivery. Electronics include mobile phones, laptops, tablets, televisions, cameras, audio devices, wearables, computer accessories and all home appliances. Large appliances such as refrigerators, washing machines and air conditioners can be returned within 10 days only if a ShopNest technician confirms a defect during installation or a service visit.

The return window is counted from the delivery date shown in your order details, not from the order date.

## Conditions for a return

Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with all tags, labels, manuals, accessories and free gifts included. Apparel and footwear must not be washed, altered or worn outdoors.

For phones, laptops and tablets, please sign out of all accounts, remove any screen lock or device lock and perform a factory reset before pickup. A device that is still locked to your account cannot be accepted and will be shipped back to you.

## Items that cannot be returned

The following items are non-returnable unless they arrive damaged, defective or wrong:

- Innerwear, lingerie, swimwear and socks
- Personalised or engraved products
- Gift cards
- Software, games and digital downloads once activated
- In-ear earphones and earbuds once the hygiene seal is opened
- Mattresses and pillows once the vacuum seal is broken
- Items marked "Final Sale" during a Big Nest Sale

## How to start a return

Go to My Orders, select the item and tap Return. Choose a reason and a pickup slot. Pickup is usually scheduled within 2 business days. Keep the item packed and ready, and hand it over only to the ShopNest courier partner, who will share a pickup OTP.

You can return some items from an order and keep the rest. Each item is handled as a separate return request.

## Return pickup fees

Return pickup is free when an item is damaged, defective or wrong. For change-of-mind returns (for example "no longer needed" or "found a better price"), a reverse-pickup fee of ₹79 is deducted from your refund. ShopNest Plus members never pay the reverse-pickup fee. Size and fit issues on apparel and footwear are best handled as a free exchange instead of a return.

## Self-ship returns

If your pincode is not serviceable for pickup, you can ship the item to our returns centre yourself within 7 days of raising the return. Send us the courier receipt and we will reimburse your shipping cost up to ₹150 as ShopNest Wallet balance.

## Quality check and refund

Returned items are inspected within 2 business days of reaching our warehouse. Once the item passes the quality check, the refund is initiated to your original payment method. UPI refunds take 1–3 business days, net banking 3–5 business days and credit or debit card refunds 5–7 business days after initiation.

If an item fails the quality check (for example it is used, damaged by the customer or missing accessories), it is shipped back to you at no cost and no refund is issued.

## Gifts

If you received an item as a gift, you can return it within the same window. The refund is issued to your ShopNest Wallet instead of the buyer's payment method, and the buyer is not notified.
