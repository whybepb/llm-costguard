# Express Delivery

## Next-day delivery

Next-day delivery is available in 25 cities, including Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Chandigarh, Kochi, Lucknow and Indore. Place your order by 12 noon to receive it the next day.

Next-day delivery costs ₹99 per order. It is free for ShopNest Plus members.

## Same-day delivery

Same-day delivery is available in 6 cities: Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai and Pune. Place your order by 10 AM to receive it the same evening, by 9 PM.

Same-day delivery costs ₹149 per order. ShopNest Plus members pay ₹49 for same-day delivery.

In these 6 cities, express deliveries also run on Sundays. In the other next-day cities, an order placed on Saturday is delivered on Monday.

## Eligible items

Express delivery is available only on items marked "Express eligible" on the product page. Furniture, large appliances and orders shipping outside India cannot be sent by express. If your cart has both eligible and non-eligible items, the order is split and only the eligible items are sent by express.

Cash on Delivery is available on express orders up to ₹10,000. Express orders above ₹10,000 must be prepaid.

## If we miss the express promise

If an express order arrives later than promised, the express fee is refunded automatically to your ShopNest Wallet within 48 hours. You do not need to contact us. The express fee is not refunded if the delay was caused by an incorrect address or because nobody was available to receive the package.

## Cancelling an express order

Express orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are shipped, like any other order. Express orders are often dispatched within an hour, so the cancellation window can be very short. If an express order is cancelled before dispatch, the express fee is refunded along with the order amount.
