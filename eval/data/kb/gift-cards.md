# Gift Cards

## Buying a gift card

ShopNest e-gift cards are available in any amount from ₹500 to ₹50,000, in multiples of ₹100. The gift card is sent by e-mail or SMS to the recipient within 15 minutes of payment, or on a date you choose when you schedule delivery.

Gift cards can be paid for with UPI, cards or net banking. They cannot be bought with another gift card, NestCoins, ShopNest Wallet balance, Cash on Delivery or EMI. Gift card purchases do not earn NestCoins.

## Validity

Each gift card is valid for 12 months from the date it is issued. The validity cannot be extended. Any balance left after the expiry date is lost, so check your balance under My Account > Gift Cards.

## Using gift cards

You can use up to 5 gift cards on a single order. Gift cards can be combined with one other payment method, such as UPI or a card, to pay the remaining amount. If the order costs less than the gift card balance, the rest stays on the card for future orders.

Gift cards can be used on any product sold on ShopNest, but not to buy other gift cards.

## Refunds on orders paid with gift cards

If you cancel or return an order that you paid with a gift card, the amount is credited back to the same gift card within 24 hours. If the gift card has already expired, the amount is added to your ShopNest Wallet instead, where it never expires.

## Gift cards are non-refundable

Gift cards cannot be returned, refunded or exchanged for cash, except where required by law.

## Lost or stolen gift cards

Treat your gift card code like cash. Once a gift card has been redeemed, it cannot be blocked or replaced. If the code has not been redeemed yet, the person who bought it can contact support with the order ID; we will block the card and reissue it once.

## Corporate gift cards

Companies buying gift cards in bulk for employees or customers can get volume discounts through ShopNest Business; see the ShopNest Business policy.
