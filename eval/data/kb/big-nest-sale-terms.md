# Big Nest Sale: Terms and Conditions

## Sale dates

The Big Nest Sale runs twice a year. The Diwali edition runs from 16 to 22 October 2026, and the New Year edition from 26 to 31 December 2026. ShopNest Plus members get early access 24 hours before the sale opens to everyone.

## Bank offer

During the sale, you get a 10% instant discount, up to ₹1,500, when you pay with a partner bank's credit card or credit card EMI on orders of ₹5,000 or more. Each card can use the offer at most 2 times during a sale. The bank offer does not apply to UPI, debit cards, net banking, Cash on Delivery or gift card purchases.

If you return part of an order that used the bank offer and the value of the items you keep falls below ₹5,000, the bank discount is recovered from your refund. If you cancel or return the whole order, you are refunded the amount you actually paid.

## Lightning deals

Lightning deals are available in limited quantities for a limited time. You can buy at most 2 units of each lightning deal. The deal price applies only while the deal timer is running and stock lasts; adding an item to your cart does not reserve it.

## Returns during the sale

Our standard return windows apply to sale purchases: 30 days from delivery for most items and 10 days for electronics. Items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged, defective or wrong.

## Price match during the sale

Neither Price Drop Protection nor Competitor Price Match applies to orders placed during a Big Nest Sale.

## Delivery during the sale

Because of high order volumes, deliveries may take 2–3 business days longer than usual during the sale. Express delivery slots are limited and may sell out.

## NestCoins during the sale

During the sale, all customers earn 2 NestCoins for every ₹100 spent, and ShopNest Plus members earn 3 NestCoins for every ₹100.

## Fair use

ShopNest may cancel orders that appear to be placed for resale, such as several orders of the same deal to the same address. If an order is cancelled for this reason, you receive a full refund.
