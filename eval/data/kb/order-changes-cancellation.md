# Order Changes and Cancellation

## Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order free of charge at any time before it is shipped. Go to My Orders, select the order and tap Cancel. You can cancel the whole order or only some items from it. Orders with the status "Order placed" or "Packed" can still be cancelled; once the status changes to "Shipped", the order can no longer be cancelled online.

Personalised or engraved products can be cancelled only within 6 hours of placing the order, because production starts soon after.

## After the order has shipped

Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled. You can either refuse the delivery at the doorstep or accept it and request a return within the return window.

If you refuse a prepaid order at the doorstep, the refund is initiated after the package returns to our warehouse, which usually takes up to 7 days. If you refuse a Cash on Delivery order, nothing is charged. Note that if 3 Cash on Delivery orders are refused within 6 months, Cash on Delivery may be disabled for your account.

## Refunds for cancelled orders

For prepaid orders cancelled before dispatch, the refund is initiated immediately. Refunds to UPI take 1–3 business days, net banking 3–5 business days and credit or debit cards 5–7 business days. Refunds to the ShopNest Wallet arrive within 2 hours. The shipping fee is refunded in full when an order is cancelled before dispatch.

## Coupons on cancelled orders

If you cancel an order before dispatch, any single-use coupon used on the order is restored to your account, provided the coupon has not expired. Bank offer discounts are not restored; you can use the offer again only if it is still running.

## Changing the delivery address

The delivery address can be changed before the order is shipped, but only to another address with the same pincode. To deliver to a different pincode, cancel the order and place a new one. The phone number for delivery can be changed until the order is out for delivery.

## Changing items, size or quantity

Items, sizes, colours and quantities cannot be changed after an order is placed. Cancel the item before it ships and place a new order. If the item has already been delivered, apparel and footwear can be exchanged for a different size or colour within 30 days.

## Rescheduling large-item deliveries

For furniture and large appliances, you can reschedule the delivery slot up to 2 times at no cost from My Orders.

## Cancellations by ShopNest

In rare cases, ShopNest may cancel an order, for example because of a pricing error, an item going out of stock or a failed address verification. You will be informed by SMS and e-mail and receive a full refund, including any shipping fee.
