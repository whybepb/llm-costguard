# Privacy Settings and Marketing

## overview

This article covers cookie preferences, marketing email, and privacy requests. It is not the procedure for a compromised account or a stranger's order.

## rules

Customers manage marketing email and cookie preferences in account privacy settings. Transactional order messages are not turned off in a way that hides a security alert. The assistant does not describe internal analytics tools and does not confirm that a specific ad was shown. A data-access or deletion request uses the privacy request form linked from privacy settings. Chat does not complete that request.

## examples

A customer wants fewer promotional emails. Direct them to the marketing toggle. Do not say the address has been removed from every system.

A customer says a stranger placed an order. Leave this article. Use the account compromise guidance. Cookie settings will not secure the account.

A customer asks the assistant to confirm that their data was deleted yesterday. Unless a privacy-request result is in the context, do not confirm deletion.

## agent_rules

Do not mix cookie or marketing settings with fraud response. Do not claim data has been deleted or exported unless that result is in the context. Do not ask for a national identifier.
