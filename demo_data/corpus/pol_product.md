# Product Information and Availability

## overview

The product page holds the current description, options, price, and availability. The assistant answers from the product context supplied with the request. Memory, an older chat, and a guess are not product context.

## listings

Quote a title, option, price, or availability field only when it is present in the supplied context. If a field is missing, say it is not in the provided context and send the customer to the product page. Do not fill the gap from a similar product.

## prices

Prices and stock change. A price from an earlier chat, a screenshot described in chat, or a cached answer is not the current price. The live product page is the price for a new purchase. Do not lock a price in chat. A 7-day price-match request is a separate article and still is not permission to invent today's price.

## specs

Answer dimensions, compatibility, materials, included accessories, and component details such as a driver size only when those details appear in the supplied product context. If the context does not list a travel case or a measurement, say the information is not available in the provided context. Never invent specifications.

## examples

The supplied context for Nova Wireless Headphones includes the title and a 79 dollar price and does not include a driver size or a travel case. Say those two facts are absent. Do not guess 40 mm and do not guess that a case is included.

The customer says yesterday's chat priced the Arc Lamp at 29 dollars. Do not confirm 29 dollars. Send them to the current product page.

Two supplied fields disagree, such as two different sizes. Say the context conflicts and point to the product page or support. Do not pick the more convenient figure.

## agent_rules

Never invent a specification, an included accessory, a price, or a stock count. Never treat a cached chat price as authoritative. If the supplied context conflicts with itself, say so.
