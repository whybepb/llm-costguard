# Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

## overview

Damaged, defective, and incorrect items use the help flow on the order. The assistant can explain what to keep and which kinds of remedies exist. It cannot approve a remedy, choose replacement over refund, or promise a shipment date.

## rules

A customer who receives a damaged, defective, or incorrect item should open Orders and select Return or Get help for that item. The flow may ask for a description and photos. Remedies can include a replacement, a return, or a refund, depending on eligibility and stock. None of those outcomes is guaranteed by this article. The customer should keep the item, the accessories, and the packaging until the flow says what to do with them.

## exceptions

A change of mind is not a damaged-item case. If the item is intact and the customer no longer wants it, use the returns article and the 30 calendar day window. A final-sale flag can block that change-of-mind return and still leave Get help available for damage when the order page shows it. A marketplace seller may have to review photos before any remedy, and immediate approval is not promised. A promo-code adjustment is not a damage remedy unless the order flow offers that amount.

## steps

Direct the customer to Return or Get help on the line item and to add photos if the page asks. Tell them to keep the item. Stop there. Do not pick replacement versus refund. Do not invent a warehouse address or a same-day dispatch.

## examples

A mug arrives cracked, Northstar fulfilled, order recent. Use Get help and photos. Say a replacement, return, or refund is possible and that the review decides which. Do not promise a replacement today.

The customer demands that replacement today. Refuse the promise. Stock and the review can change the outcome.

The mug is the wrong color and is otherwise intact. It is an incorrect-item report, not a partial promo refund. Use the same order flow.

The customer says the mug is not damaged and they changed their mind 10 days after delivery. Leave this article. The 30 calendar day return rule applies if no final-sale flag is present.

A cracked mug that is also final sale and sold by a marketplace seller still starts in Get help. Do not promise the refund, and do not ignore the seller review.

## agent_rules

Do not guarantee a replacement, a refund, or a same-day shipment. Do not tell the customer to discard the item. Do not ask for a card number, CVV, or one-time passcode as part of the photo review.
