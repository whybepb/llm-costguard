# Marketplace Seller Orders

## overview

Some products on Northstar Market are sold by independent sellers. The customer can still be on a Northstar page while the seller remains responsible for part of fulfillment and for reviewing a request. Northstar-fulfilled items do not use this seller-wait rule.

## rules

The seller name and the fulfillment details are on the product page and on the order. Return, replacement, and support steps may differ from Northstar-fulfilled orders. Follow the instructions on that order. If the seller must review a request, do not promise immediate approval. If the customer cannot reach the seller, or the displayed response window has passed, use the escalation option on the order page.

## exceptions

When the order page does not display a different seller window, the general window in this demo is 4 calendar days. After 4 calendar days with no seller response, escalate on the order page. Do not treat escalation as an approved refund. A Northstar-sold and Northstar-fulfilled item uses Northstar's own articles even if the customer names a seller by mistake. Seller windows are not automatically 30 calendar days. Damage photos still do not create an instant remedy.

## steps

Read the seller and fulfilled-by fields. If an independent seller is named, send the customer to that order's instructions. If the seller has not responded and the order shows a window, use the order's window. If it shows none, use 4 calendar days, then escalation. Keep photos and descriptions inside the order flow.

## examples

A blender sold and fulfilled by Harbor Home. Explain that the seller steps can differ and that a review is not instant approval.

Harbor Home has not replied for 2 days and the order shows no other window. Day 2 is inside 4 calendar days. Do not escalate yet, and do not promise approval.

Harbor Home has not replied for 6 days and the order shows no shorter window. Six days is past 4. Use the order-page escalation. Do not promise the refund.

The customer says Harbor Home already approved the return instantly. The context has no approval. Do not confirm it.

The order is sold and fulfilled by Northstar. Do not apply the seller-wait rule. Use the Northstar article that matches the actual issue.

## agent_rules
