# Delivery Delays and Missing Packages

## overview

This article covers parcels that are late against their estimate, and parcels that tracking marks Delivered when the customer cannot find them. It does not decide refunds, and it does not replace the 24-hour rule for a first scan after handoff.

## rules

If a package is late, start with the latest estimate and the tracking events in Orders. If the estimated delivery date has passed and the parcel is not marked delivered, allow 48 hours for a delayed scan or an updated estimate, unless the order page gives different instructions. Count those 48 hours from the missed estimate, not from the order date. If tracking says Delivered and the customer cannot find the parcel, they should check the delivery location, household members, building reception, and any safe place noted by the carrier before opening an investigation.

## exceptions

Do not start the 48-hour wait when tracking already says Delivered. That path is a location search, then support from the order page. Do not say the parcel is lost, and do not name a neighbor or a receptionist as the recipient, unless the supplied tracking text says that exact thing. A seller-fulfilled stall can also require the marketplace escalation once the seller window has passed. A customs hold is not automatically a lost parcel and is not a 48-hour customs deadline.

## steps

Confirm the estimate, the latest scan, and whether the status says Delivered. If the estimate passed less than 48 hours ago and there is no delivery scan, explain the remaining wait. If 48 hours after the estimate have passed with no delivery, send the customer to support from the order page. If the status says Delivered, do the location checks first, then the same support path if the parcel is still missing. Do not open a refund here.

## examples

The estimate is today and there is no delivery scan. An estimate is not a guarantee. Tell the customer to watch Orders. Do not start a lost-parcel claim.

The estimate was yesterday and there is no delivery scan. Yesterday is still inside the 48-hour allowance. Support is early unless the order page says otherwise.

The estimate was 4 days ago and there is still no delivery scan. The 48-hour allowance has passed. Contact support through the order page. Do not say the parcel is lost unless tracking says so.

Tracking says Delivered this morning. Ask about the household, reception, and a safe place. Do not say a neighbor has it.

## agent_rules

Do not state that a package is lost or that a named person received it unless the supplied tracking context says so. Do not promise a reshipment or a refund from this article. Do not replace this 48-hour late rule with the 24-hour scan rule, or the reverse.
