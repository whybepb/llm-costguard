# Returns and Eligibility

## overview

Northstar Market accepts returns of most physical items for a limited time after delivery. This article is the general rule for items sold and fulfilled by Northstar. It states how the window is counted, what condition the item should be in, and which exceptions override the calendar. The assistant may explain the rule and the steps. The assistant cannot approve a return in chat. A specific order can be stricter than this article. When the order page and this article disagree, the order page is the source of truth.

## eligibility

Most physical items can be returned within 30 calendar days of the delivery date shown on the order. Counting starts on the delivery date, not the order date, and weekends count. Day 30 is the last day to start a return. On day 31 the general window is closed. The item should be unused, with the original tags, accessories, and packaging that came with it. Wear, missing pieces, or a consumed product can block a return even when the calendar window is still open. The customer starts at Orders, selects the item, and chooses Return item.

## exceptions

A product marked final sale or non-returnable on the product page stays non-returnable even inside the 30 calendar days. An independent marketplace seller can impose different steps and a different window. Those orders follow the instructions on that order, not this article alone. An international shipment can require the customer to pay return shipping. That cost does not change the 30-day count. A damaged or incorrect item can use the help flow on the order even when a change-of-mind return is blocked. This article does not set refund timing.

## steps

Open Orders and select the purchase. Choose the item, then Return item, then a reason. Use only the label or drop-off method the page displays. Do not ship the item to an address copied from a chat. Keep the confirmation. A submitted request is not an approved return and is not a posted refund.

## examples

Nova Wireless Headphones delivered 10 days ago, sold by Northstar, not final sale, unused, with the case and cable: the customer is inside the 30 calendar days and may submit a return. Say that approval appears only when the order page confirms it.

The same headphones delivered 40 days ago: day 40 is outside the window. Do not tell the customer the standard path is still open. They may look at the order page for a listed exception, but the general rule is closed.

A lamp delivered 5 days ago and marked final sale: the calendar window is open, and the final-sale exception still blocks the return. "Only 5 days" is not enough.

A blender sold by Harbor Home: do not apply this 30-day rule as if the seller had adopted it. Use the seller instructions on the order.

## agent_rules

Do not promise that a return is approved. Do not invent a longer window, a free label, or a restocking fee. Do not treat the customer's description of condition as proof of eligibility. Do not quote a 7-day price match, a 14-day review edit, a 24-hour tracking scan, a 48-hour late-package wait, or a 5 to 10 business day card refund as if that number were the return window.
