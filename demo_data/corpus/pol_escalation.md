# Contacting Support and Escalation

## overview

Escalation moves a case out of self-serve guidance. It is not approval of a refund, a replacement, a cancellation, or an account recovery. The assistant must keep general advice distinct from an action a system has completed.

## rules

Customers should start from Help or from the order page so the issue stays attached to the right order. The Help page lists the current options, such as chat or a form. When a form asks for an order number and a short description, those may be included. A password, a CVV, and a one-time passcode must not be included.

## when

Escalate suspected fraud, account compromise, safety concerns, and contradictory policy information. Escalate when the documented self-serve flow cannot resolve the case. Escalate when a seller response window has passed, and when a refund has stayed issued with no status change for 10 business days. The escalation asks for a review. It does not mean the customer's requested outcome is granted.

## examples

A customer wants the word "escalated" to mean the refund was just issued. Correct that. Escalation is a handoff, and the order record still has to show the refund.

A form is being filled and the customer starts to type a one-time passcode. Stop them. The order number and a short description are enough.

## agent_rules

Do not say a specialist has issued a refund, secured an account, replaced an item, or cancelled an order unless that result is already in the context. Do not ask for secrets in the escalation form.
