# Price Match

## overview

Price match has its own short clock. It is easy to confuse with returns because both can be counted from delivery. The numbers are different, and a price match is not a return.

## rules

A price-match request must be submitted within 7 days of delivery, for an identical in-stock Northstar offer, and only when the current terms say price match is open. The customer submits it from the order's help flow. Approval is not automatic. The 7-day price window is not the 30 calendar day return window, not the 5 to 10 business day card-refund estimate, and not the 10 business day stale-refund check.

## examples

A lamp delivered 6 days ago can be submitted for price match when the terms are open and the other offer qualifies. Do not promise approval.

A lamp delivered 20 days ago is outside this 7-day window. It may still be inside the 30 calendar day return window. Do not use the return window to grant a price match, and do not use the price window to refuse a return.

A missed code such as SAVE10 is not repaired by calling it a price match after the order is placed.

## agent_rules

Do not call 7 days the return period. Do not promise a price match. Do not invent a competitor price.
