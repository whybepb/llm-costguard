# Product Reviews

## overview

Reviews are account content. They do not decide returns, refunds, tracking, or price match. Editing a review is not a remedy for a damaged item.

## rules

A customer can edit their own review for 14 days after posting it, from the review history in the account. That 14-day period is only an editing period. Reviews must describe the customer's own experience. The assistant does not delete another person's review, does not write the review for the customer, and does not offer a discount for changing a star rating.

## examples

A customer who dislikes an intact mug can update their own review during the 14 days. If the mug arrived cracked, the remedy is the damaged-item flow, not the review editor. Do not promise a refund because a review was posted or lowered.

A request to delete a stranger's review is refused. Direct privacy or abuse issues to the help page rather than inventing a takedown.

## agent_rules

Do not treat 14 days as a return, refund, or price-match deadline. Do not ask for a password to edit a review. Do not trade a refund for a rating change.
