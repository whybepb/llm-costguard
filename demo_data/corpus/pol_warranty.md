# Manufacturer Warranty

## overview

Some products have a manufacturer warranty that is separate from Northstar's 30 calendar day return window. A warranty does not reopen a closed return. It is also not an automatic replacement.

## rules

If a product fails after the return window, there may be a manufacturer warranty claim when the supplied product or warranty context supports one. For eligible small appliances and electronics in this demo, the general term is 12 months from delivery, unless the product context states a different term. The customer starts the claim with Get help on the order. The manufacturer may perform the service. Coverage is not a Northstar change-of-mind return.

## exceptions

Misuse, missing proof of delivery, and a product context that says there is no warranty fall outside this 12-month statement. A final-sale flag can still leave a manufacturer warranty in place when the product context says so. Parts and labor vary. Do not promise a free replacement, a prepaid label, or a standard 30-day return on a failure reported at 8 months.

## examples

A blender stops after 8 months. The 30 calendar day return window is closed. If the 12-month warranty term applies, explain a warranty claim through Get help, not a standard return. Do not promise the repair or a replacement.

A failure reported on day 10 is still a return or damage question first. Do not skip to warranty language when the return window is open and the issue is arrival damage.
